# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a respectful, welcoming, and
harassment-free experience for everyone. Contributors and maintainers commit to
making decisions based on the technical and community needs of the project,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or
expression, experience level, education, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that helps create a constructive community include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- respecting different viewpoints and experiences;
- accepting constructive feedback in good faith;
- focusing discussion on what improves the project and its users; and
- showing empathy toward other community members.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or personal attacks;
- sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, deliberate disruption, or bad-faith technical discussion;
- publishing another person's private information without permission; and
- any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that
do not follow this Code of Conduct. Depending on the circumstances, they may
also issue a warning or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

To report a concern, contact Mercado Pago through the private
[Developer Support channel](https://www.mercadopago.com/developers/support).
Do not use a public issue for a report that includes personal information or
would make someone unsafe. Security vulnerabilities follow the separate
[security policy](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests, and
other project communication spaces. It also applies when someone represents the
project in public.
